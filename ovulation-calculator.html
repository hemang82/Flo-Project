Key takeaways on calculating your fertile window

Whether you are trying to conceive or simply wanting to understand your body better, knowing your fertile window is essential. A standardized medical algorithm can pinpoint these days using your cycle data.

How are your fertile days calculated?

A typical menstrual cycle lasts 28 days. Ovulation usually happens about 14 days before your next period starts. Because sperm can live inside the reproductive tract for up to 5 days, your fertile window is generally the 5 days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

What are the signs of ovulation?

  • Changes in cervical mucus: Discharge becomes clear and stretchy.
  • Mild pelvic pain: Slight twinge on one side.
  • Basal body temperature shifts: A slight drop followed by a spike directly after ovulation.
