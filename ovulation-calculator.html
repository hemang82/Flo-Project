Key takeaways on calculating your fertile window

Whether you're trying to conceive or simply wanting to understand your body better, knowing your fertile window is essential. A standardized medical algorithm can pinpoint these days using your cycle data.

How are your fertile days calculated in the ovulation calculator?

A typical menstrual cycle lasts 28 days. Ovulation—the release of an egg from the ovary—usually happens about 14 days before your next period starts. Because sperm can live inside the reproductive tract for up to 5 days, your fertile window is generally the 5 days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

What's the average length of a menstrual cycle?

While 28 days is the textbook average, a healthy cycle can range anywhere from 21 to 35 days. Tracking your period for a few months is the best way to find your unique average.

What are the signs of ovulation?

  • Changes in cervical mucus: Discharge becomes clear and stretchy (like raw egg whites).
  • Mild pelvic pain: Also known as mittelschmerz, a slight twinge on one side.
  • Basal body temperature shifts: A slight drop followed by a spike directly after ovulation.

Can ovulation tracking increase your chances of pregnancy?

Yes. Having intercourse during your fertile window maximizes the chance of conception. Using our Period Tracker App allows you to log daily symptoms, making predictions even more accurate.
