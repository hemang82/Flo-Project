How are your fertile days calculated in the ovulation calculator?

A typical menstrual cycle lasts 28 days, but healthy cycles can range anywhere from 21 to 35 days. Ovulation—the release of an egg from the ovary—usually happens about 14 days before your next period starts.

Because sperm can live inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, and an egg lives for 12 to 24 hours after being released, your fertile window is generally the 5 days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself. Our calculator does this math for you instantly.

What's the average length of a menstrual cycle?

The global average is 28 days, but anything between 21 and 35 days is considered completely normal for adult women. It is also completely normal for your cycle to vary by a few days from month to month due to stress, diet, or lifestyle changes.

What are the signs of ovulation?

While a calculator provides a great mathematical estimate, your body also gives physical clues when ovulation is approaching. Paying attention to these signs can help you confirm the calculator's prediction:

  • Changes in cervical mucus: Your discharge may become clear, slippery, and stretchy (like raw egg whites).
  • Mild pelvic pain: Also known as mittelschmerz, you might feel a slight twinge or cramping on one side of your lower abdomen.
  • Basal body temperature shifts: Your resting temperature drops slightly before ovulation, then spikes directly after.
  • Increased libido: Natural hormonal shifts often cause a surge in sex drive leading up to ovulation.

Can ovulation tracking increase your chances of pregnancy?

Absolutely. Having intercourse during your fertile window maximizes the chance of sperm meeting the egg.
