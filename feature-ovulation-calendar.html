Key Biomarkers for Tracking Ovulation

Combining calendar calculations with your body's physical signals gives you the highest fertility prediction accuracy.

LH Surge Testing

Luteinizing Hormone spikes 24-36 hours before egg release. Simply snap or log test strip results to mark your peak fertility day.

Basal Body Temp (BBT)

A slight thermal shift of 0.5°F - 1.0°F occurs right after ovulation due to rising progesterone, confirming egg release.

Cervical Mucus Changes

As estrogen peaks, cervical fluid turns clear and stretchy (egg-white texture), providing an optimal environment for sperm survival.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ovulation, fertility windows, and conception timing.

The fertile window comprises the 5 days before ovulation plus ovulation day itself (total 6 days). Because sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, intercourse during this window yields the highest odds of pregnancy.

For regular cycles, calendar calculation provides up to 90%+ statistical accuracy. When combined with LH test strip logging or BBT tracking, accuracy approaches near 99% for pinpointing egg release.

While the calendar identifies high-risk fertile days, natural fertility awareness methods require strict secondary biomarker logging (BBT & cervical mucus). We recommend using reliable barrier protection during your fertile days if preventing pregnancy.

Our intelligent machine learning model adapts to your historical cycle variations. If your luteal phase or overall cycle length fluctuates, the app dynamically shifts your predicted fertile window.
