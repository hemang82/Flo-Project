How is my due date calculated?

The most common way to calculate your pregnancy due date is by taking the first day of your last normal menstrual period (LMP) and adding 280 days (40 weeks).

This assumes you have a regular 28-day cycle. If your cycles are longer or shorter, your doctor may adjust this date later during an ultrasound scan. This is known as Naegele's rule.

Can my due date change?

Yes! It's important to remember that your due date is just an estimate. Only about 4% to 5% of babies are actually born on their exact due date. Most babies arrive between 37 and 42 weeks of pregnancy. An early ultrasound (usually around 8 to 12 weeks) is the most accurate way to date a pregnancy and may change your initial EDD.
