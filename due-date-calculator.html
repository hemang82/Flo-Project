Understanding Your Pregnancy Due Date

Discovering you are pregnant marks the beginning of an extraordinary 40-week journey. Our free online pregnancy due date calculator estimates your expected date of delivery (EDD) based on Naegele's Rule—the clinical gold standard method recognized by the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG).

Calculating your EDD early helps healthcare providers track fetal development milestones, schedule critical prenatal screening tests (such as first-trimester screening and anatomy scans), and monitor your baby's growth timeline accurately.

How Is Your Pregnancy Due Date Calculated?

Obstetric pregnancy timeline is officially measured from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP), rather than the exact day conception occurs. Because ovulation typically happens 14 days into a standard 28-day cycle, you are technically considered 2 weeks pregnant at the moment of fertilization!

The Formula Behind Naegele's Rule:

EDD Formula: [First Day of Last Period] + 7 Days - 3 Months + 1 Year

Example: LMP of May 10th → Add 7 days (May 17th) → Subtract 3 months = February 17th of the following year.

If your average cycle length is shorter or longer than 28 days, our calculator automatically adjusts the calculation by adding or subtracting the variance to maintain clinical precision.

Can Your Due Date Change?

Yes, it is very common! In fact, clinical research shows that only about 5% of babies arrive on their exact calculated due date. Most healthy full-term deliveries take place between 37 weeks (early term) and 42 weeks (late term).

Reasons Your Healthcare Provider May Adjust Your EDD:

  • Early Ultrasound Dating Scan: During your first-trimester ultrasound scan (typically performed between weeks 8 and 13), your doctor measures the crown-rump length (CRL). If the scan date differs by more than 5 to 7 days from your LMP estimate, the ultrasound date becomes your official clinical due date.
  • Irregular Menstrual Cycles: If you experienced delayed ovulation or irregular period timing before conceiving, calendar formulas based on period start date may over- or under-estimate fetal age.
  • Fundal Height & Milestone Observations: Abnormal fundal height measurements or early ultrasound anatomical findings may warrant timeline recalibration.

Clinical Dating Scan Advice

Online calculations are standard biological estimations. Always confirm your clinical pregnancy timeline with your obstetrician or midwife during your routine prenatal appointments.
