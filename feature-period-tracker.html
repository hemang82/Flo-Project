Understand Your 4 Cycle Phases

Your body transitions through distinct hormonal phases each month. Tracking helps you align your energy, nutrition, and exercise accordingly.

Phase 1

Menstrual Phase

Days 1 – 5

Estrogen and progesterone drop. Focus on rest, hydration, and light movement like walking or yoga.

Phase 2

Follicular Phase

Days 6 – 13

Estrogen rises rapidly. Experience higher mental focus, increased stamina, and vibrant mood boost.

Phase 3

Ovulatory Phase

Days 14 – 16

Luteinizing Hormone (LH) surges. Peak fertility window for conception planning or natural tracking.

Phase 4

Luteal Phase

Days 17 – 28

Progesterone spikes then falls. Track PMS symptoms, cravings, and prepare your body for the next cycle.
